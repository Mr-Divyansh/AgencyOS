# AgencyOS — User Flows

## 1. Agency Administrator Flow (Sarah Lin)
1. **Login & Overview**: Logs in or switches to Admin persona. Enters Dashboard and reviews active KPIs, revenue trends, and upcoming deadlines.
2. **Project Management**: Views Projects, checks completion percentages, creates a new engagement for a client, and assigns team members.
3. **CRM Pipeline & Conversion**: Navigates to CRM pipeline, advances a qualified lead to "Won", and triggers the 1-click "Convert to Client" dialog to automatically provision a Client record and initial Project.
4. **Task Delegation**: Reviews the agency Kanban board, prioritizes urgent deliverables, and reassigns tasks.
5. **Team & Agency Settings**: Inspects member workload, updates team roles, and adjusts agency preferences.

## 2. Team Member Flow (Alex Rivera)
1. **Task Execution**: Views personal assigned tasks on the Kanban board.
2. **Status Updates**: Drags/moves task from "In Progress" to "In Review" or "Done".
3. **Collaboration**: Checks project briefs and timeline deadlines.

## 3. Client Portal Flow (Marcus Vance @ Acme Corp)
1. **Client Workspace**: Logs in as Marcus Vance (Acme Corp).
2. **Project Visibility**: Views Acme Corp's active brand refresh and web platform deliverables.
3. **Deliverable Review**: Reviews deliverable milestone preview, approves or submits feedback / change request.
4. **Billing Transparency**: Inspects invoice records (Paid vs Pending) and due dates.
